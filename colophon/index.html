Afterword

How this edition was made

The text. Autobiography of a Yogi by Paramahansa Yogananda, first edition, 1946. Its United States copyright was never renewed, so the book entered the public domain in 1975. No copyright is claimed over it here, and none could be.

The narration. All 49 narrated chapters — 17.3 hours — were spoken by Kokoro-82M, an open-weights text-to-speech model released under Apache-2.0. The voice is synthetic. No human read this book aloud, and nothing here should be mistaken for a performance by a person. Each chapter's audio is timed paragraph by paragraph, which is what lets the text follow along as it reads.

The edition and this site. Directed and assembled by John Records — the selection and writing of the annotations, the footnote apparatus, the design of the reader, and the production of the narration. He is not the editor of Yogananda's text: not a word of the Autobiography has been altered, abridged or rearranged. What was added is the apparatus around it.

The pictures. Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under their own licences and are linked from the footnote where each is referenced.

The licence. The annotations, the narration and this site are released under CC0 1.0 (creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0): copy, share, remix, republish or sell them, for any purpose, without permission and without credit.

The source. The full build — text, annotations, narration and the scripts that made them — is at github.com/john-records/autobiography-of-a-yogi.
